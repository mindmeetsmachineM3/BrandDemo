# Brand intake: what to collect from the client

Send this list on WhatsApp. Put the answers in `brand.json` and the files in `assets/`.

**Files**
- Logo (PNG, transparent background is best) -> `assets/logo.png`
- 3 to 10 clear product photos (plain background is fine) -> `assets/products/`

**Facts (we only write what is on this list)**
- Brand name, Instagram handle, one phone / WhatsApp number to show on posts
- Tagline, if any
- What they sell and who buys it
- For each product: name, one line description, 3 real benefits
- Current offers or prices we are allowed to mention (leave empty if none)
- Language for posts (English, Hinglish, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali) and how the brand should sound
- Brand colours (hex codes if they have them; otherwise take them from the logo)
- Dates that matter in the next 30 days (festivals, launches, sale days)
- Do and don't list: words, topics, styles to avoid
- Anything special they asked for -> `extra_prompt`
